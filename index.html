About

Friendly on mic, focused off it

I'm Joanna, a Bay Area voice actor with a warm, upbeat sound that feels like talking to a friend. I've always loved bringing characters to life, and some of my favorite performances happen at home, doing voices for my son's characters. Whether it's an animated character, a commercial, or calm narration that helps people slow down and unwind, I bring that same warmth and fun to every read.

Off the mic, I'm an attorney and law school director in Silicon Valley, where I lecture on law, direct a high tech law program, and deliver remarks at national legal conferences. Speaking clearly and warmly to an audience is part of my everyday life.

Both sides shape how I work. I bring playfulness to every read and preparation to every project. I read scripts closely, adapt quickly to direction, and treat every deadline as a priority, so you get a creative partner and a reliable professional.
